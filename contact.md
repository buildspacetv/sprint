# Contact

- **During the event** — the [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/nN58zxSTFR) is where coordination happens; host
  engineers are on the floor all day.
- **Registration** — [Luma](https://luma.com/nkknxvrz). Space is limited and every attendee is approved.
- **Finding a team** — post in the [team directory](https://physical-ai-sprint.vercel.app/teams.html), even solo.
- **A problem with this site** — open an issue at [https://github.com/opencolin/physical-ai-sprint-handbook/issues](https://github.com/opencolin/physical-ai-sprint-handbook/issues).
- **Building against the data** — see [https://physical-ai-sprint.vercel.app/developers.html](https://physical-ai-sprint.vercel.app/developers.html);
  no credential is required.
